Case study / Service business MVP

Fame Lux Nails & Beauty

A refined service website concept for a beauty studio, designed to become a productized booking and lead-capture package.

Open live demo ↗ View source ↗
AudienceIndependent nail and beauty studio
RolePositioning, UX, visual system, frontend build
StackHTML, CSS, JavaScript, conversion UX
StatusLive frontend MVP with productized next step
01Problem

Small beauty studios need trust, clarity, and booking intent within the first visit.

Many local service websites look generic, hide the offer, and push customers back to phone or social DMs. The commercial opportunity is a focused website that improves enquiry quality and prepares the business for booking automation.

02Solution

A brand-first service page with a clearer path to enquiry.

  • First viewport communicates the atelier category and premium service feel.
  • Service presentation supports scanning before a customer decides to enquire.
  • Visual direction is tailored to beauty rather than using a generic SaaS layout.
  • The next technical layer is lead capture, appointment request storage, reminders, and admin follow-up.
03Product offer
01

Booking-ready landing page with service menu and enquiry flow.

02

Simple dashboard for leads, customer notes, status, and follow-up reminders.

03

Optional automation for confirmation emails, review requests, and weekly reports.

04Trade-offs

The current demo sells the visual direction, not yet the full operating workflow.

The next build should connect the contact journey to a backend lead table and admin view. Payment deposits and calendar sync are valuable, but only after the booking request flow is proven.

05Commercial step

Package this as a repeatable website plus mini-CRM for salons and creative studios.

This is the strongest first sellable product direction in PRJ008: concrete customer, obvious workflow pain, small MVP, and a clear upgrade path from landing page to operations tool.
